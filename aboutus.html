Our Story

Made for Your Everyday Moments

Sweet Clover Bakery opened its doors in 2019 with a small menu, a big love of baking, and a goal of becoming a gathering place for the local community.

Founder Emma Carter grew up baking with her grandmother and eventually turned that childhood tradition into a career. After studying culinary arts and working in several professional kitchens, Emma returned to northwest Missouri to open her own bakery.

What started as a small neighborhood bakery has grown into a local destination for fresh pastries, artisan breads, custom cakes, and seasonal desserts.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Still wondering about something? Give us a Call!

Do you accept custom orders?

Yes. We offer custom cakes, cookies, cupcakes, and dessert boxes for a variety of occasions.

How far in advance should I place a custom order?

We recommend placing custom orders at least seven days in advance.

Do you offer gluten-free products?

We offer select gluten-free items. Because our bakery handles wheat and other allergens, we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Do you offer delivery?

At this time, Sweet Clover offers bakery pickup. Delivery may be available for select large orders by special arrangement.

Can I request a specific cake flavor?

Yes. Customers can select from our standard flavors or discuss a custom flavor with our team.

Do you make wedding cakes?

Yes. Wedding cakes and dessert tables are available by consultation.

Do you accept same-day orders?

Some items are available for same-day purchase depending on availability. Custom orders require advance notice.

How can I place an order?

Visit the bakery, call us, or submit the online custom-order request form.
